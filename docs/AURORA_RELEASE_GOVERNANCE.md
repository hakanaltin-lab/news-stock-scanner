# AURORA Research and Release Governance v1.0

Effective: 2026-10-26

Normal strategy and policy releases occur on Monday, with one consolidated weekly release note. Infrastructure, data-quality, security, and critical bug fixes may be committed outside the Monday window. Automated evidence/report commits are not strategy releases.

Weekly review covers production health, data quality, fresh-forward evidence, SEC and broker-data progress, turnover-guardrail diagnostics, unresolved risks, and next-week targets.

Live strategic-policy review is quarterly unless a predefined safety condition requires earlier intervention. V8.4.8 independent-cohort evidence continues on its own clock.

Price-only cross-sectional factor research is frozen. No new price-only factor search, horizon search, weight optimization, or threshold optimization is allowed on the already-used 2012-2021 or 2022-2026 datasets.

Permitted active work: SEC EDGAR/XBRL point-in-time data, broader forward evidence capture, data-quality controls, operational turnover controls, IBKR paper read-only account/position/cash visibility, execution-safety infrastructure, and later dead-code cleanup.

Any new strategy hypothesis requires a materially new information source or hypothesis, a named protocol, pre-registered features/rules, a genuinely independent validation period, fixed PASS/FAIL criteria before results are read, and no automatic live promotion.

Paper order submission and live execution remain blocked.
