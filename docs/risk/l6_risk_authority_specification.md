# L6 Risk Authority Specification v1.0

## Purpose

L6 Risk Authority is the independent risk control layer of AURORA AI CIO.

Its responsibility is not to optimize returns.

Its responsibility is:

> Protect capital by preventing unacceptable risk exposure.

No CIO decision, Alpha score, market opportunity or human approval can override an L6 BLOCK decision.

---

# 1. Risk Authority Principles

## Core Rules

1. Risk has independent veto authority.
2. No position can bypass predefined limits.
3. Every trade must pass L6 validation.
4. Risk limits tighten during adverse market regimes.
5. Capital preservation has priority over opportunity capture.
